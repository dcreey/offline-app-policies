# Support

These apps run entirely on your device — there's no account, no server, and no sync, so there isn't much that can go wrong on the backend, because there is no backend.

If something isn't working, you found a bug, or you have a question or feature request, email:

**dcreey@gmail.com**

Include the app name and, if you can, your device model and iOS version — that's usually enough to reproduce most issues.
